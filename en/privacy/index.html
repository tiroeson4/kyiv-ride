About this policy

This policy explains the information the app needs and how you can manage it. The app helps people find free lifts or share a taxi in Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast. Participants arrange the ride, taxi booking and payment themselves.

Account and profile

Sign-in processes your account identifier and authentication information, including email. Your sign-in email is not published. Your name, photo, chosen name colour and crown may be visible to other users in profiles, rides and chats.

Contacts and vehicle

Your phone, Telegram, Viber and vehicle details are optional. External contacts are available to you and authorised participants of a shared ride. A direct conversation alone does not disclose them. Vehicle details may appear with a participant’s profile.

Route and map

We store chosen places, addresses, times and ride participation to support search and ride arrangements. We do not use GPS or background tracking. Google Maps, Places and Geocoding process search queries and selected map points to find addresses. A manually chosen point may contain precise coordinates. Favorites are private and sync across your devices.

Who can read messages

The Kyiv chat is public, including to guests. Ride chats are available to authorised participants; direct conversations are available to the two participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. An administrator may review reported content. Named feedback is linked to your account; anonymous feedback has no account link. Avoid including personal information if you want to remain anonymous.

Retention

Ride history and its group chat remain for 30 days after closure. Direct conversations and Kyiv chat messages have no automatic expiry. Deleting a conversation for yourself hides it only for you. Reports remain for 30 days; feedback remains until an administrator deletes it. Google address coordinates are privately cached for up to 30 days and may be refreshed. Favorite names and place identifiers remain until the place or account is deleted.

Phone notifications

Delivery uses your device token, platform, language and sign-in session link through Expo, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service. Notifications contain generic event descriptions, without conversation text or external contacts. You can disable each category in Settings.

Advertising and Premium

Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. Requests depend on available consent settings and request non-personalised ads. Technical information is still processed. Active Premium disables advertising. Google Play or App Store handles payment; the app does not receive card numbers. RevenueCat checks subscriptions using a separate technical identifier that differs from your public profile ID.

Storage and protection

Supabase provides sign-in, database, files and server functions. Connections use HTTPS and server permissions restrict private information. Service providers may process logs and technical information under their own policies. Transmission and storage cannot be guaranteed entirely free of risk.

Account deletion and requests

Delete your account in app settings or on the public deletion page after signing in. The server removes your profile, photo, contacts, favorites, created rides and messages; direct conversations involving the account are deleted for both participants. Anonymous feedback without an account link will remain after account deletion until an administrator deletes it. Account deletion does not cancel store subscriptions. Contact support to request access to, correction or deletion of your data.
