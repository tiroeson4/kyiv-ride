Privacy

Developer and data controller: Андрій Офат
Support email: kyiv.rides.dev@gmail.com

Account data

Authentication processes your account ID and identity provider information, including email. Your sign-in email is not published. Your name, photo, name color, and crown may be visible to other people in profiles, rides, and chats.

Contacts and rides

Telegram, Viber, and phone are optional. They are visible to you and authorized participants of a shared ride. A direct conversation alone does not disclose contacts. Vehicle information may appear with a participant's profile. Selected places, addresses, times, and ride participation are stored to support search and ride arrangements.

Maps and places

GPS and background location tracking are not used. Typed search queries and the selected map area are processed by Google Maps / Places and our server. Manually selected points can contain precise coordinates. Favorites are private and synchronized across your devices.

Messages and feedback

The Kyiv chat is public, including to guests. Ride chats are available to authorized participants; direct chats are available to the two participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. An administrator can review reported content. Named feedback is linked to your account; anonymous feedback does not retain that link. Avoid including personal information in feedback if you want to remain anonymous.

Retention

Ride history and its group chat are kept for 30 days after closure. Direct conversations and Kyiv chat messages have no automatic expiry. Clearing a conversation for yourself hides it only for you. Reports are kept for 30 days; feedback remains until an administrator deletes it. Google address coordinates are cached privately for up to 30 days and may be refreshed. Favorite names and place IDs remain until the place or account is deleted.

Notifications

System notifications use a stored device token, platform, language, and session link. Delivery uses Expo, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Apple Push Notification service. Push notifications contain generic event descriptions, without conversation text or external contact details. All categories can be disabled in settings.

Ads and subscription

Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information. Ad requests depend on available consent settings and request non-personalized ads. This still involves technical data processing. Premium removes advertising. Stores process payments; the app does not receive card numbers. RevenueCat verifies subscription information using a separate technical identifier that is not your public profile ID.

Services and protection

Supabase provides authentication, database, files, and server functions. Connections use HTTPS; server permissions protect private data. Providers may process service logs and technical information under their own policies. Access to private information is enforced beyond the user interface.

Deletion and requests

You can delete your account in settings or through the browser deletion page after signing in. The server removes your profile, photo, contacts, favorites, created rides, and messages. Direct conversations involving the account are deleted for both participants. Anonymous feedback without an account link may remain. Deletion does not cancel store subscriptions. Contact support to request access, correction, or deletion of your data.
