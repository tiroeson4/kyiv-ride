Community rules

  • Respect other people. Threats, harassment, hate speech, sexual content, scams, and spam are prohibited.
  • Post real rides and keep available seats up to date. Mark your ride as started or cancel it when your plans change.
  • Do not publish someone else's phone number, documents, home address, or other personal information without permission.
  • Lifts are free. Shared taxi participants arrange their own taxi booking and payment. The app does not collect ride payments.
  • Long-press an unwanted message to report it or block its author. An administrator may remove content and restrict an offender's access.
  • Check your arrangements and choose a safe meeting point. The app helps you find people to travel with; it does not verify drivers' identities or guarantee a ride. Contact the appropriate services in an emergency.

Subscription terms

Premium is a monthly subscription: no ads, a name color of your choice and a crown beside it. Google Play or App Store displays the exact price and currency before payment. The subscription renews monthly until you cancel it in the store. Cancellation stops the next charge; benefits remain until the end of the paid period unless the store revokes the purchase. Uninstalling the app or logging out does not cancel your subscription. The store handles refunds. Restore purchases using the same app and store accounts. Benefits are available on other devices when you sign in to the same app account. A subscription does not change ride participation rules.
