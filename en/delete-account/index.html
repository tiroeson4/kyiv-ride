Delete account

In the app: Profile → Settings → “Help & data” → “Delete account”.

If you cannot sign in, email support from your account address with the subject “Account deletion”. We delete the account after checking that the request came from that address. tiroeson4@gmail.com

Your profile, photo, contacts, favorites, rides you created, and messages will be deleted. Your direct conversations will also disappear for the other participants. Active rides will be cancelled; you will leave other rides. Anonymous feedback without an account link may remain.

Anonymised daily statistics counters without an account link are kept.

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. If you have one, cancel it in Google Play or the App Store to avoid another charge.

Without the app: sign in below with your account email and password, then confirm deletion.
